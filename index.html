How well does my fall and jump model detect?

My part of a team project: a YOLO11n pose model, 7 classes, 100 epochs, trained on 15,000+ images. Validation set, all 7 classes.

0.716recall (bounding box)
0.583mAP50 (bounding box)
0.537precision (bounding box)
7classes, incl. fall and jump

Box detection vs pose keypoints

Finding the person (box) works much better than placing body keypoints (pose).

What it means

Recall is higher than precision, which suits a safety alert: better to flag a possible fall for review than to miss one. The next step is more varied "not fall" footage to cut false alarms.

Sales prediction

Python · pandas · scikit-learn · Streamlit

How does advertising spend on TV, radio and newspaper relate to sales?

A Random Forest regression model trained on advertising spend, evaluated with MAE, MSE, RMSE and R².

Key finding: the model explained 98.1% of sales variation on 40 held-out markets (R² 0.981). TV and radio drive the predictions, while newspaper spend adds almost nothing (importance 0.013).

Car price prediction

Python · pandas · scikit-learn · Streamlit

Which features of a used car drive its selling price?

A Random Forest regression model trained on 301 used-car listings, with one-hot encoding for fuel type, seller type and transmission, evaluated with MAE, RMSE and R².

Key finding: on 61 held-out cars the model reached R² 0.964, off by 0.60 lakhs on average. A car's new price alone carries 0.88 of the model's importance.

Can a camera feed raise an alert the moment someone falls, a fire starts, a weapon appears or a fight breaks out?

Final year team project that detects fire, falls, weapons and fights in live video and logs each event for review.
